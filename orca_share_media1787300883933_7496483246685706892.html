ABOUT ME

A simple life, meaningful dreams.

I am MD MEHDI HASAN, a student and teacher who comes from a simple family and believes in dreaming beyond limitations. I enjoy setting meaningful goals and making sincere efforts to turn those dreams into reality.

I value simplicity, patience, respect, and good relationships with people. I try to live a balanced life, stay connected with those around me, and remain patient when facing challenges. My faith in Allah gives me strength and keeps me hopeful while I move forward.

One of my greatest aspirations is to do something meaningful for my family, relatives, neighbors, and people who struggle in life. I hope that, by the grace of Allah, I can use my knowledge and abilities to contribute positively to society and to the country.

“I want to earn people's love, live with purpose, and move forward with faith.”
